Traffic across 100 intersections, twelve city zones, one congestion model.

This dashboard replays the results of the Smart City Traffic Analytics big-data pipeline: an S3-simulated MapReduce job, a PySpark KMeans congestion classifier, GPT-2 scenario forecasting, a STRIDE threat model for the IoT sensor layer, and a future-hotspot predictor.
